## Research question

A role-asymmetric debate protocol that balances clinical finding recall and false-positive control using retrieved cases as shared evidence anchors.

## My contribution

- Proposed the role-asymmetric dual-agent framework and structured debate protocol
- Implemented the BiomedCLIP, FAISS, and top-K evidence retrieval pipeline
- Designed ablations around accuracy, recall, and inference-cost trade-offs

## Results and boundaries

BLEU-4 0.122 → 0.260; Macro-F1 0.368 → 0.437; 3 image calls + 4 text steps. Only public, sanitized evidence is shown; manuscripts under review are not distributed.
