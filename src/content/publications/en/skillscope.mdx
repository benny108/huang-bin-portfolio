## Research question

Procedural skills can transfer workflow knowledge to small language agents, but a full skill is not necessarily safe for every target model. SkillScope asks how much of the same skill should be exposed to a fixed student before there is enough evidence to support deployment.

## Method

- Decompose a candidate skill into semantic blocks and construct full, prefix, semantic-selection, and random exposure conditions under explicit token budgets
- Freeze the student model, agent harness, task distribution, and executable verifier so that model, tool, and evaluator changes do not enter the comparison
- Record response surfaces, negative transfer, non-monotonicity, random controls, and failure signatures for the target student

## Results and boundaries

Across SWE-Skills-Bench, full skills show negative transfer in 7 of 15 model–family pairs. In the 210-row Qwen3-4B audit, the best candidate partial exposure reaches 83.41% versus 73.94% for the full skill; across 19 expanded SkillsBench tasks, sub-full random controls match or exceed full skills on all 19.
