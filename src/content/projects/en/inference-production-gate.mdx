## Problem

A fast single run is not production readiness. Image and video generation must also meet constraints on memory, quality, text stability, audio-video consistency, and long-duration reliability.

## Image benchmark

Under the same Qwen-Image, 1024×576, and 8-step setup, I compared two quantized inference paths. Generation time decreased from 2.284 s to 0.972 s—about 2.35×—while resident memory, peak memory, and quality trade-offs were reported together.

## Video benchmark

I evaluated an 8-step LTX-2.3 22B distilled GGUF Q4 text-to-audio-video pipeline. Short warm runs averaged 19.541 s, but subtitle artifacts, long dialogue, and black-frame failures did not pass a strict production gate.

## Conclusion

The outcome is framed as a performance–quality boundary study rather than a completed production delivery. The gate makes optimization claims reproducible, challengeable, and actionable.
